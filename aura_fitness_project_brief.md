# Project Brief: AURA FITNESS Landing Page

## Project Overview
AURA FITNESS is a new feminine activewear brand launching with a minimalist, high-end aesthetic. The goal is to create a professional landing page that showcases products and drives sales leads directly to WhatsApp, bypassing the need for a complex e-commerce checkout system in the initial phase.

## Brand Identity: Essence Aura
- **Vibe:** Clean, luxury, sophisticated, and high-performance.
- **Visual Language:** Heavy use of whitespace, minimalist typography (Hanken Grotesk), and a monochromatic palette with subtle grey containers.
- **Core Values:** Premium Quality, Extreme Comfort, and Unique Style.

## Target Audience
- Women interested in fitness and wellness.
- Customers looking for "feel-good" activewear that transitions from the studio to the street.
- Users who prefer personalized shopping experiences via direct messaging (WhatsApp).

## Key Features & Requirements
### 1. Landing Page Structure
- **Navigation:** Clean top bar with category links (Tops, Leggings, Conjuntos, Sobre) and functional icons (Search, Account, Bag).
- **Hero Section:** High-impact editorial imagery with a clear brand statement and a "Shop Collection" call to action.
- **Category Navigation:** Visual tiles for "Shop the Essentials" (Tops, Bottoms, Accessories).
- **Product Showcase:** "Hot Right Now" section featuring product cards with price, status badges (Novo, Sold Out), and WhatsApp-specific CTAs.
- **Value Propositions:** Icon-based section highlighting premium quality, comfort, and style.
- **Footer:** Informational links (Terms, Privacy, Returns) and direct support contact.

### 2. Lead Generation (WhatsApp Integration)
- Instead of a "Buy Now" or "Add to Cart" button that leads to a checkout page, each product features a "Comprar via WhatsApp" button.
- The intention is to initiate a direct conversation with the seller to finalize the purchase and discuss payment/shipping.

### 3. Responsive Design
- The site must maintain its "luxury" feel across both desktop and mobile devices.
- Mobile optimization is critical as the primary traffic source for new activewear brands is often social media (Instagram/TikTok).

## Content Strategy
- **Copy:** Professional, inspiring, and concise. Focus on the "feel" and "performance" of the pieces.
- **Imagery:** High-resolution, editorial-style photography featuring diverse models in active/lifestyle settings.
- **Language:** Brazilian Portuguese (PT-BR).

## Technical Implementation
- **Framework:** HTML5 / Tailwind CSS.
- **Components:** Modular sections for easy updates to the product catalog.
- **Platform:** Static landing page (Stitch generated).

## Success Metrics
- Visual alignment with high-end e-commerce standards (e.g., Shopify luxury templates).
- Clear and functional "WhatsApp" lead path.
- Mobile performance and readability.
